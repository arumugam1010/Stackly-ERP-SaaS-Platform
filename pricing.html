Pricing FAQ

Are there any setup fees?

No, there are no hidden setup fees for the Starter, Professional, or Business plans. Enterprise implementation fees vary based on complexity.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Prorated charges will be applied automatically.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes, we offer a 30% discount for registered non-profit organizations. Contact our sales team to apply.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and ACH/Wire transfers for annual Business and Enterprise plans.
